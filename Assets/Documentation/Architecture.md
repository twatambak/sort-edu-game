The game is called "Onde Vai?" (Where it Goes?). It's a sorting game aimed at toddlers and kids in the age between 2 and 5. The goal of the game is to help kids understand the concept of sorting things out and putting them in scenarios where they need to sort objects into two groups. While the main mechanic remais the same, the idea is to add variety by presenting the players with different and varied scenarios such as:
1. Separating LETTERS and NUMBERS
2. Separating TOYS and TOOLS
3. Separating HEALTHY FOODS and UNHEALTHY FOODS
4. Separating GOOD BEHAVIOR and BAD BEHAVIOR
5. Separating VOWELS and CONSOANTS
6. Separating FARM ANIMALS and JUNGLE ANIMALS

Some of the art is AI generated and it still needs to be properly sliced and adapted to the game. 
The game is targeted for cellphones and web.

The goal is to have two different game modes:
1. Drag and Drop: players can use the touchscreen to drag the objects to the correct location.
2. Question: players can choose if its either GROUP A or GROUP B

One particular guideline is that the game must be played without the need of text, as it's aimed to kids that have not gone through complete alphabetization yet.

The game should have database integration and allow teachers and parents to visualize their kids process directly throught the game. 
The game must be well polished and highly understandable.
