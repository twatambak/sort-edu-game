The game is called "Onde Vai?" (Where it Goes?). It's a mobile sorting game aimed at toddlers and kids in the age between 2 and 5. 
The goal of the game is to help kids understand the concept of sorting things out and putting them in scenarios where they need to sort objects into two groups.
